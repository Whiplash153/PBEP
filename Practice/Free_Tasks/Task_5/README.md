# Task: Employees Older Than 30

## Condition
The HR department received a list of employees and their ages.  
You need to display the names of all employees who are older than 30 and show how many such employees there are.

## Solution
We used a **dictionary** to store employee names and ages.  
Then applied a **list comprehension** with a condition to filter employees older than 30.  
Finally, we printed their names and the total count.

**Tools used:** dict, list comprehension, condition (if), len(), print.
